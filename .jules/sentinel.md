## 2024-05-24 - Missing PAM Authentication for Screen Lockers
**Vulnerability:** The active screen locker (`hyprlock`) lacked an explicit PAM authentication definition in the NixOS configuration (`security.pam.services.hyprlock = {};`), while `swaylock` was defined twice. Without this configuration, the locker may fail to authenticate users properly, leading to potential lockout or fail-open (bypass) states depending on the PAM fallback behavior.
**Learning:** In NixOS, screen locker binaries are not automatically granted PAM authentication rights. Any screen locker in use (like `hyprlock` alongside `swaylock`) requires a direct and explicit declaration in the configuration to function securely.
**Prevention:** Whenever introducing or configuring a new screen locking mechanism or display manager in NixOS, explicitly map out its PAM requirements and verify its presence in `security.pam.services`. Ensure no redundant definitions inadvertently omit other active lockers.
